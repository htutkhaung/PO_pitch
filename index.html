Product Owner (PO) Dimension

  • Mandate: Owns the Vision, Backlog, and Business ROI.
  • Scope: Enterprise-wide authority above all pods.
  • Decision Power: Final decision-maker on what gets built or discarded.
  • Level of Experience: Requires deep multi-architecture, financial, and operational mastery.

Business Analyst (BA) Dimension

  • Mandate: Documents requirements detail and workflow mechanics.
  • Scope: Pod-level; analyzes specific features inside a single sub-unit.
  • Decision Power: Investigator / Specifier — does not make commercial product decisions.
  • Execution: Reports tickets to the PO and Dev Leads for approval.
